ABOUT US

At EYECARE, we’ve got an eye for style—and we care for your vision. Shop glasses, sunglasses, contact lenses, and eye-care solutions, all in one place. Book an eye exam, update your prescription, or get your frames repaired. Our friendly team is here to make caring for your vision simple while helping you find eyewear that fits your needs and personal style. From clearer vision to your perfect look, we’ve got you covered—eye to eye.

FAQS

Quick answers to common questions about our eye care, eyewear and orders.

How do I book an eye exam?

Go to the Services page, choose a date and time, enter your name, phone number and service, then press Book Now.

How often should I have my eyes checked?

Many people have an eye exam about once every one to two years, or sooner if your vision changes or you have eye discomfort. Our team can advise you based on your needs.

Can I update my prescription?

Yes. Choose Prescription Update when you book, and our team will check your vision and update your prescription.

What if I need to change or cancel my appointment?

Please contact us as early as you can so we can free the slot for someone else and help you find a new time.

How do I choose frames that suit me?

Each frame on our Frames page lists the face shapes it works best with. You can also visit us and our friendly team will help you find a fit and a style you like.

Do you repair frames?

Yes. Book a Frame Repair service or visit us with your frames, and we will check what can be fixed.

Do you sell contact lenses and eye drops?

Yes. We carry contact lenses, lens solutions and lubricant eye drops, along with eyeglasses, sunglasses and eyewear cases. Browse contact lenses.

How do I buy something?

Press Purchase on any product to add it to your cart. Open the cart from the shopping cart icon to review your items and check out.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is ₱ 100, and it is free for orders of ₱ 2,000 and up.

Do you have promo codes?

Promo codes can be entered in the Order Summary on the cart page. Follow us for current offers.

Still need help?
